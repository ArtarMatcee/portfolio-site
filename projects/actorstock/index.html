UX/UI case study

ActorStock

A Thai casting marketplace. Production companies search for performers by the attributes a role is actually written in — build, skin tone, languages, stunt and dance skills — then invite the ones they want by private code.

Explore the project → See the journey
Role
UI/UX design and prototyping
Project type
Redesign of the existing ActorStock website
Delivery status
Interactive prototype; not implemented

Project overview

One product, two sides of a casting call

The product
A Thai casting marketplace: production companies look for performers, performers publish what they can do. Both sides run on one mobile product and read from the same structured talent profile — measurements, appearance, languages, special skills and credits.
The problem
A casting brief is written in attributes — twin sisters, 12–18, Thai, slim, able to speak English on camera — and a marketplace organised around names and photographs cannot answer it. Performers have the opposite problem: the skills that would get them cast are the ones with nowhere to go.
My responsibility
UI/UX design and prototyping, as a redesign of the existing ActorStock website.

Who it is for

Registration asks for a category before anything else: ศิลปินนักแสดง (performers) or แคสติ้งจัดหางาน (casting & recruitment). That single choice decides every screen that follows.

What the design covers

  • Registration, PDPA consent, profile creation
  • Attribute search, shortlists, invitation codes
  • Job posting and applicant tracking
  • Job search, job detail and applications
  • Actor Study video lessons
  • Subscription plans and checkout

Platform

Mobile, 440 pt wide — thirteen screens for the casting side and nineteen for the performer side, including a nested profile set.

Main user journey

From a casting brief to an invited performer

Problem
Casting companies and performers want opposite things, which is an argument for two products with two sign-ups. I wanted one, because the two sides only have value to each other.
Decision
Design one path that crosses both: a casting company narrows the roster to one person and hands them a code that is meant to become their account. One email-and-password form serves both sides, because the category chosen at registration already decided which product you are in.
Constraint
The handover has to work for someone who does not have an account yet — so the proposed flow makes the invitation code the sign-up itself rather than a message about one.

    One door for both sides

    A single email-and-password form serves performers and casting companies. Nothing on it asks which you are — the category picked at registration already decided that.

  3. 03

    Results are designed to be counted and sortable. Each card carries the four facts a casting call screens on — gender, age, height, build — plus the work types that performer accepts, and a profile-completeness score.

    Social reach sits above the fold, then identity and measurements, then appearance and special skills in two columns, then the photo gallery. The screen offers exactly one action.

Home & plans

How the product sells itself

Three screens carry acquisition: the home page a performer lands on, the plan table it funnels into, and the checkout that closes it.

Problem
A performer arriving cold has no reason yet to believe there is work here, or to pay for a plan. I treated the home page as the place that has to earn both.
Decision
Make the home page evidence before pitch: the audience numbers and a rail of postings — shoot date, location, budget, each with its own Apply Now — come before any claim about the platform.
Constraint
Ten phone-screens of scroll is a long ask, so it asks for the sign-up exactly twice: Register Now at the top and a newsletter field at the very bottom.

A home page doing the work of a whole marketing site

Ten phone-screens of scroll that open on a casting call and close on a wall of sponsor logos. It asks for the sign-up twice and no more: Register Now at the top, a newsletter field at the very bottom.

1

Proof arrives before the pitch

Directly under the hero sit the YouTube and TikTok cards — 1.5M+ subscribers and 800K+ followers, each with a Follow action. The audience is evidenced before anything is claimed about it.

2

Job postings, not pictures of jobs

100 Jobs has been announced leads a rail of postings drawn in full — shoot date, location and budget, each with its own Apply Now. The marketing page is designed to be the product as well. The postings shown are sample content, not live listings.

3

Price is the twelfth block, not the first

Plans & Offers only appears after the value propositions, the audience segments and Actor Study — once the page has earned the question.

Two numbered steps that never leave the screen

The checkout counts Select a Plan as step one and Complete Your Purchase as step two — then puts both on the same 2,227 pt scroll. No wizard, no page between choosing a tier and paying for it.

1

The tiers are a carousel, not a wall

FREE, TALENT PRO at ฿99 and TALENT PREMIUM at ฿199 scroll horizontally on a navy band, so a 440 pt screen never has to shrink three feature lists to fit.

2

The discount is asked for before the card

The coupon field sits above payment, so the number in the summary is the number actually charged — no correction after the card details are in.

3

Feature deep dives

Six decisions that carry the product

Each of these earns its screen space: they are what a casting company is paying for, and what a performer is optimising their profile against.

Problem
A casting brief asks for attributes, not names. I took search built around names and photographs as the thing to replace, and a biography-style profile as giving it nothing to match on.
Decision
Turn the brief into the interface. Ten multi-select attribute groups and three numeric ranges make “Thai or Chinese, slim or average build, speaks English” one query, and the performer’s profile is filled in with the same controls, so the two cannot drift apart.
Constraint
A growing query has to stay readable on a phone: chips fill coral when active, and Filter and Reset close the panel so a long brief is built in peace and applied once.

Search in the language a casting brief is written in

A brief never says "user ID 75909". It says twin sisters, 12–18, Thai, slim, who can speak English on camera. The filter panel is a direct translation of that sentence.

1

Ten attribute groups, all multi-select

Ethnicity, gender, skin tone, hair, build, distinguishing features, language, style, special skills and accepted work. Chips fill coral when active, so the query stays readable as it grows.

2

Three ranges for the things that are numbers

Age 0–50 years, height 140–200 cm, weight 40–100 kg, each a dual-handle slider with its current span printed above it.

3

One commit, one escape

Nothing re-queries mid-edit. Filter and Reset close the panel, so a long brief is built in peace and applied once.

A result card you can reject without opening it

Casting is mostly elimination. The card is built so that the decision to keep looking costs one glance, not one tap and a back button.

1

The count is the feedback

100 Profiles sits at the top of the list with a Newest sort beside it — the filter panel's only confirmation that it did something.

2

Four facts, in the same place every time

Gender, age and height on one line; build on the next; accepted work types on the third. Identical geometry across every card makes the column scannable vertically.

3

Profile completeness is public

A 10/10 คะแนนโปรไฟล์ badge on the card tells the casting side how much to trust the data — and gives the performer a visible reason to finish their profile.

Reach, facts, skills, faces — in that order

The profile is stacked in the order a casting decision is actually made, and it ends in a single button rather than a menu.

1

Audience before appearance

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook follower counts occupy the first band of the screen. For brand and influencer work that number is the casting criterion.

2

Appearance and ability are separated

ลักษณะ holds ethnicity, languages, skin, hair and build; ความสามารถพิเศษ holds martial arts, dance, singing. Two columns, so a brief can be checked against each without re-reading prose.

3

One action, and a QR code to leave with

The only control is สนใจโปรไฟล์นี้ — add to shortlist. A QR code in the identity card carries the profile out of the app and into a production meeting.

A private door into a public marketplace

Agencies bring their own talent. The invitation code lets a company seed the platform with people it already represents, without opening a back channel.

1

One code, one email, one use

A generated code such as ACVIP-6092 is bound to a guest email address at creation, and carries a status from the moment it exists.

2

The sent list is the audit trail

Below the form, every issued code shows its recipient and whether it is still ยังไม่ได้ใช้งาน — unused — with a control to delete it.

3

Arrival costs one field

The invited performer never sees a registration form. The code screen is the sign-up, which is what makes the handoff worth building.

The performer's side filters on money and travel

The same two-screen pattern as the casting side — search, then detail — but the facets change completely, because what varies between roles is not what varies between people.

1

Two filters, not twenty

Budget range and province. A performer decides on fee and how far they will travel; the twenty attribute facets belong to whoever is casting them.

2

Top Opening Jobs skips the search

A coral card of the five most-posted roles — #1 แม่ครัว through #5 ตัวประกอบ — sits above the filters, so the common case is one tap rather than a query.

3

The brief is the page

Job detail carries the real casting call in full — eligibility, what to submit, per-file size limits — with Apply Now pinned above it and related roles below.

Posting a role, then watching it fill

The other half of the casting account: put a role out, then track it without opening it.

1

One form, eight fields

Title, agency, brief, location, shoot date, budget, headcount and an image upload. Save (บันทึกงาน) and clear (ล้างฟอร์ม) are told apart by colour, not position.

2

The count is the status

Each posted role shows จำนวนที่สมัคร as 3/99 — applications against the cap — so a role that is not filling is visible from the list.

3

Everything else is on the card

Active status, ดูรายชื่อผู้สมัคร to open the applicant list, and a shareable job link — no detail screen needed to manage a posting.

The rest of the file

Screens that carry the product without carrying an argument — open sign-up, the profile editor, the lesson library, and the shell both account types share.

Design system

Coral, teal, navy and one pill radius

Problem
Two account types, six feature areas and a bilingual interface, with plenty of room to drift apart screen by screen.
Decision
Hold them in tokens rather than habits — one pill radius, one control height, a fixed type ramp — and read the values below straight from the file’s own variables rather than measuring them off a screenshot.
Constraint
DM Sans carries no Thai glyphs, so Thai falls back to the system face. The ramp has to hold up across both scripts.
